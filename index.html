How to use this

  • DCA means buying a fixed amount on a regular schedule.
  • The more averages a coin is below, the better the long-term value right now.
  • The idea is simple: the deeper the value, the larger the DCA.

What the three averages are

  • 2Y: the average weekly close of the last 2 years. Price drops below it first.
  • 3Y: the same idea over 3 years, sitting in the middle.
  • 200W: about 4 years of weekly closes. Dropping below it is the rarest and deepest.

Good to know

  • The charts use weekly closes, and the last point is the live price.
  • BTC, LTC and DOGE update live.
  • Not financial advice.
