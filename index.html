How it works

The chart shows price relative to its 200-week SMA, the average weekly close of roughly the last four years.

PADDLE≤ 1× → DCA in
RIDE1× – 2× → HODL
KICK OUT≥ 2× → DCA out

The 200W SMA filters out short-term swings and works as a slow long-term mean.

Historically, most major cycle lows have traded at or below it.

Zones show where price sits, not when it will turn, and one zone can last months or years, so DCA spreads your buys or sells instead of guessing the bottom or top.
